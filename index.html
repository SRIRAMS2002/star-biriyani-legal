Privacy Policy

Star Biriyani · Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what information Star Biriyani ("we", "us") collects when you order from us or message us on WhatsApp, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name and phone number (including your WhatsApp number)
  • Delivery address, if you choose delivery
  • Order details: items, amounts, date and time
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp

How we use it

  • To take, prepare and deliver your orders
  • To send order updates (accepted, ready, delivered, cancelled)
  • To reply to your questions and support requests
  • To send offers, only if you have opted in. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with:

  • Delivery partners, who receive your name, phone number and address to deliver your order
  • Payment providers, to process payments
  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries our messages under its own privacy policy

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for business and legal purposes, then delete them.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to view, correct or delete your data at any time. Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com or message us on WhatsApp with "Delete my data". We will act on your request within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure.
